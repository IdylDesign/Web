Building
Permits
Did you know that even for remodels
that seem to be minor the local
building department requires you to
submit a set of CAD plans to obtain a
permit? Most interior design firms
will present to the homeowners a
beautiful rendering of the completed
project but that is not a set of
plans that you can use to apply for a
remodel permit. With our expertise in
CAD plan production we will develop
for you a set of plans that will
comply with the drafting standards
required by the building department.
You can directly use the set we will
develop for you to apply for the
remodel permit. With our services
there is no need for you to hire and
coordinate with a separate drafting
consultant. Saving money and effort..
Survey of existing structure – our services include a survey of the
existing structure and lot that are required to be presented in the
permit application set of plans. No need to hire a separate consultant
for this.
It is our experience that in the process of production of the CAD
drawings several details usually come to surface that need attention of
the interior designer. With our service you will completely avoid the
need to coordinate between the designer and the CAD production
consultant. We guarantee that what you have agreed on with us as the
interior designers we will show on the CAD permit drawings. This
eliminates potential omissions and need to re-submit the permit
application. Saving time and money down the road.
Even replacing an existing window with a slightly larger opening requires
a calculation by a structural engineer. This is understandable, since the
shear walls that provide the main strength during earthquakes will be
weakened by any opening in them. We can connect you with a structural
engineer that we have worked and coordinated with on other projects. We
will seamlessly transfer the structural details developed by the engineer
to the CAD drawings required for the permit application.

About Us

Design from lived experience

We've been remodeling the homes we live in for nearly 30 years. We transform real-life lessons into thoughtful, durable designs that balance beauty, function, and budget.

Our design philosophy

We design for how people actually live. We focus on creating cohesive whole-home stories, reducing needless complexity, and selecting materials that age gracefully.

Cohesion
Every update should belong to the whole-house story so rooms never feel disconnected.
Simplicity
Fewer, well-chosen finishes produce calmer, longer-lasting results.
Timelessness
Proven natural materials and classic details over passing trends.
Utility
Ample storage, clear circulation, and practical daily-use solutions.

Common remodel pitfalls we solve

  • Overwhelming decisions that delay projects or raise costs.
  • Remodel projects that look great in isolation but clash with the rest of the home.
  • Overcomplicated material palettes that confuse rather than harmonize.
  • Small functional issues - door swing obstructions, missing storage, poor workflow that undermine everyday use.

Budget-friendly phasing and strategy

When a whole-house remodel isn't feasible, we break work into prioritized, manageable phases that advance a long-term vision while protecting your budget. Each stage adds functional value and preserves future options.

Why work with us

We combine lived experience, practical problem solving, and a keen eye for lasting design. We help you avoid common pitfalls, make better decisions up front, and get results you'll love for years.

Quick tips

Practical details matter

  • Limit number of surface finishes for visual calm and harmony
  • Choose natural, proven, timeless materials for longevity
  • Plan door swings, appliance, and furniture clearances early for ease of access
  • Do not skip over seemingly unimportant details and do not let them just figure out by the contractor as they choose.
  • You are about to pay substantial sum of money so better make it right the first time.
